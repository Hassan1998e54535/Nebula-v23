name: NEXUS product branding
description: The app's visible product name should consistently be NEXUS rather than inherited Fluxer branding.
---

Use NEXUS for user-facing product identity across the app, including the desktop window and screens that display the instance product name. Do not erase legally required license notices, source attribution, or technical identifiers merely to remove visible upstream branding.

**Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the installed app's Fluxer title and asked that the NEXUS name appear across all screens.

**How to apply:** Normalize legacy upstream product-name values at the shared client boundary so the change reaches screens consistently; keep legal and internal references separate from user-facing branding.
